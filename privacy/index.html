Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Terragotcha ("we", "us") handles information when you use terragotcha.com (the "Site"). Terragotcha is a free, interactive 3D world globe and geography quiz.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information. The Site stores a small anonymous device token in your browser's local storage so your quiz progress and daily-challenge streak persist on your device. This token is not linked to your identity.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand, in aggregate, how the Site is used (for example which quizzes are popular). Google Analytics may set cookies or use similar identifiers to measure sessions and interactions. This data is processed by Google on our behalf.

Advertising

The Site may display advertising provided by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and you can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your consent choices

When required by law (for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), we present a consent message before advertising and analytics storage is enabled, using Google's certified consent management. Until you consent, analytics and advertising cookies are set to a denied state. You can change your choice at any time using the consent controls presented on the Site.

Data retention and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Aggregate analytics and advertising data are handled by Google under its own privacy terms. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Children

Terragotcha is a general-audience educational Site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the Site operator via the contact details published on terragotcha.com.
